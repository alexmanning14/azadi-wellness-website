About

Hi, I’m Sami.

I’m Samira, a licensed psychotherapist in the DMV area, Reiki practitioner, and lifelong student of the connection between mind, body, and spirit.

With over a decade of experience in mental health, I’ve seen how much growth is possible when we begin to understand ourselves beyond the surface. While traditional approaches to mental health offer valuable tools, I’ve always felt called to explore the deeper layers of healing — the patterns we carry, the energy we hold, and the parts of ourselves that are ready to evolve.

Azadi Wellness was born from that vision and from my personal journey: to create space for a more holistic approach to personal growth, one that honors both the science of psychology and the wisdom of our inner world.

Through coaching, Reiki, intuitive practices, and wellness consulting, I help people reconnect with themselves, move through what’s holding them back, and create a more aligned way of living and working.

My approach blends clinical insight, holistic practices, and intuitive exploration to support meaningful change — not just in how you feel, but in how you show up in your life.

Azadi means freedom in Farsi. To me, that means freedom to grow beyond old patterns, trust yourself more deeply, and become who you’re ready to be.

I’m so glad you’re here, and I’m honored to be a part of your journey. Welcome to Azadi Wellness.

With love,
Sami
